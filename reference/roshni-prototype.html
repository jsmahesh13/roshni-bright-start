The arithmetic of being overlooked.

In an Indian government classroom, one teacher can hold forty to sixty children. Attention is finite — so some kids simply slip through, not from neglect but from numbers.

30–35:1
the legal pupil–teacher norm (RTE). Real government classrooms often run 40–60 : 1.

The forbidden list

  1. Never diagnose or use clinical vocabulary.
  2. Never predict a future or assign a risk score.
  3. Never rank a child against peers.
  4. Never assign a character label.
  5. Never speak to, or be reachable by, a student.
  6. Never speculate about a home or family.
  7. Never recommend a disciplinary consequence.
  8. Never send a parent anything a teacher hasn't approved.
